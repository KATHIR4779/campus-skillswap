Getting Started

How do I register for Campus SkillSwap?

To register:

  1. Click the "Register" button on any page
  2. Fill in your details including your university email
  3. Verify your email address
  4. Complete your profile setup
  5. You'll receive 2 free Time Credits to start!

What universities are supported?

Currently, we support students from:

  • Hindustan Institute of Technology and Science (HITS)
  • PSG College of Technology
  • Coimbatore Institute of Technology (CIT)
  • And other universities in Coimbatore

Only university email addresses are accepted for registration.

How does the Time Credit system work?

Time Credits are our virtual currency:

  • You earn credits by teaching skills to other students
  • You spend credits to learn skills from other students
  • Each skill has a credit cost per hour (1-5 credits)
  • New users get 2 free credits to start
  • No real money is involved - it's all about time exchange

Account & Profile

How do I update my profile information?

To update your profile:

  1. Go to your Dashboard
  2. Click on "Profile Settings"
  3. Update your information
  4. Save changes

Can I change my university email?

No, your university email cannot be changed as it's used for verification. If you need to update it, please contact support.

How do I reset my password?

To reset your password:

  1. Go to the login page
  2. Click "Forgot Password"
  3. Enter your university email
  4. Check your email for reset instructions
  5. Follow the link to create a new password

Skills & Teaching

How do I add a skill to teach?

To add a new skill:

  1. Go to your Dashboard
  2. Click "Add New Skill"
  3. Fill in the skill details
  4. Set your availability and pricing
  5. Submit for review

What types of skills can I teach?

You can teach any skill you're knowledgeable about, including:

  • Academic subjects (Math, Science, Engineering)
  • Programming and Technology
  • Languages (Tamil, English, Hindi, etc.)
  • Arts and Creative skills
  • Life skills (Cooking, Fitness, etc.)
  • Sports and Physical activities

How do I set my skill pricing?

Skill pricing is based on Time Credits per hour:

  • 1 Credit: Basic skills, simple tutorials
  • 2 Credits: Intermediate skills, structured lessons
  • 3 Credits: Advanced skills, specialized knowledge
  • 4-5 Credits: Expert-level skills, intensive training

Consider your skill level and the value you provide when setting prices.

Sessions & Learning

How do I request a learning session?

To request a session:

  1. Browse the Marketplace
  2. Find a skill you want to learn
  3. Click "Request Session"
  4. Choose your preferred time
  5. Send your request to the teacher

Where do sessions take place?

Sessions can take place:

  • On campus (libraries, study rooms, cafeterias)
  • Online via video call
  • At safe public locations near campus
  • At the teacher's or student's residence (with mutual agreement)

Always prioritize safety and meet in public places when possible.

What if I need to cancel a session?

To cancel a session:

  1. Go to your Dashboard
  2. Find the session in "Upcoming Sessions"
  3. Click "Cancel Session"
  4. Provide a reason for cancellation
  5. Notify the other party as soon as possible

Cancellations within 24 hours may result in credit penalties.

Time Credits

How do I earn Time Credits?

You earn credits by:

  • Teaching skills to other students
  • Completing teaching sessions successfully
  • Receiving positive reviews from students
  • Participating in special events and challenges

How do I spend Time Credits?

You spend credits by:

  • Requesting learning sessions
  • Booking sessions with teachers
  • Participating in premium workshops

Credits are automatically deducted when you book a session.

What if I run out of credits?

If you run out of credits:

  • Start teaching skills to earn more credits
  • Complete your profile to get bonus credits
  • Participate in community events
  • Refer friends to the platform

Remember: teaching is the primary way to earn credits!

Messaging

How do I message other students?

To send messages:

  1. Go to the Messages page
  2. Click "New Message"
  3. Search for the student you want to contact
  4. Type your message and send

Can I send files or images in messages?

Currently, you can:

  • Send text messages
  • Share links and resources
  • Use emojis and basic formatting

File sharing features are coming soon!

How do I report inappropriate messages?

To report inappropriate messages:

  1. Open the message conversation
  2. Click the "Report" button
  3. Select the reason for reporting
  4. Provide additional details if needed
  5. Submit the report

Our moderation team will review the report promptly.

Troubleshooting

I can't log into my account

Try these steps:

  1. Check your email and password for typos
  2. Make sure you're using your university email
  3. Try resetting your password
  4. Clear your browser cache and cookies
  5. Try using a different browser
  6. Contact support if the problem persists

The website is loading slowly

To improve loading speed:

  • Check your internet connection
  • Close unnecessary browser tabs
  • Clear your browser cache
  • Try using a different browser
  • Disable browser extensions temporarily

I'm not receiving email notifications

Check these settings:

  1. Verify your email address in your profile
  2. Check your spam/junk folder
  3. Add noreply@campusskillswap.com to your contacts
  4. Check your email notification preferences
  5. Contact support if emails are still not arriving

Safety & Guidelines

What are the safety guidelines for meeting in person?

For your safety:

  • Always meet in public places initially
  • Inform a friend or family member about your meeting
  • Trust your instincts - if something feels wrong, leave
  • Don't share personal information beyond what's necessary
  • Report any suspicious behavior immediately

What should I do if I feel unsafe?

If you feel unsafe:

  1. Remove yourself from the situation immediately
  2. Report the incident through the platform
  3. Contact campus security if on campus
  4. Reach out to support for assistance
  5. Consider involving local authorities if necessary

What content is not allowed on the platform?

Prohibited content includes:

  • Inappropriate or offensive language
  • Harassment or bullying
  • Spam or promotional content
  • Academic dishonesty (cheating, plagiarism)
  • Any illegal activities

Violations may result in account suspension or termination.
